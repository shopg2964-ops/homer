Privacy that's on by default, not an upsell.

A messaging app is only as good as the trust behind it. SummitJambo treats every conversation as private until you choose otherwise.

End-to-end encryption, always onMessages and calls are secured in transit, with no server-side access to your content.
PIN & two-step verificationAdd a second layer of protection so your account can't be taken over by your number alone.
Chat lockKeep your most personal conversations behind fingerprint or face unlock, tucked into their own folder.
Privacy checkupA guided walkthrough of your settings so you choose your protection level deliberately, not by default.
Practical knowledge & skills application centre

Where studying stops being theoretical.

Built on Summit International Institute's model — most of the work is doing, not reading. Every student leaves having made real progress on a real problem.

30% Theory
70% Practical Application
01

Discover a problem worth solving

Start from a real issue affecting real people — not an attractive solution in search of a problem. Name who's affected and what happens if it stays unsolved.

02

Research, map, and match

Pull knowledge from your own field and beyond it. See how others across the country, continent, and world have approached the same problem, then map what you know against what you still need.

03

Build, test, and take feedback

Narrow to your strongest approach, set a timetable, and use AI and other tools where they genuinely help. Test the solution, take feedback that sharpens it, and set aside the feedback that doesn't.

04

Launch and keep improving

Ship the finished solution, tell people it exists, and treat the feedback that follows as the start of the next version — not the end of the project.

The connectivity mission

Signal from the air, for the five billion still waiting to get online.

SummitJambo grew out of a simple ambition: build affordable, license-free wireless mesh networks — grounded in local hardware, solar power, and community operators — so the cost of a connection never stands between an idea and the person who needs it, starting with the youth of Africa.
